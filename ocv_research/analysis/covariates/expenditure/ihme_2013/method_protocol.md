# Prespecified IHME 2013 expenditure sensitivities

Status: optional exploratory analysis, separate from main model. This protocol is written before fitting either expenditure model. Do not edit `final_output/`, the frozen manuscript, or the existing income sensitivity.

## Estimand and two fixed specifications

The unit and estimation period remain the balanced 25-country × 66-month panel, July 2014–December 2019. Let `Y_cm` be the indicator for at least one dashboard-classified reactive OCV request. Let `A_cm` count outbreak-record active months in the **12 months m−12 through m−1**, excluding the outcome month; concurrent records contribute separately, and start/end months are inclusive. Define `Z_cm = ln(1+A_cm)`, `B_c = mean_m Z_cm` over the same 66 months, and `W_cm = Z_cm − B_c`. These are the untouched frozen predictors.

For each expenditure definition `h`, fit a **separate** pooled country-month logistic regression:

`logit Pr(Y_cm=1 | B_c,W_cm,H_c^h) = α_h + β_B,h B_c + β_W,h W_cm + γ_h H_c^h`,

where `H_c^government = ln(1+ghes_per_cap_ppp_mean_c,2013)` and `H_c^total = ln(1+the_per_cap_ppp_mean_c,2013)` from the updated September 2019 IHME *Global Health Spending 1995–2016* release. Values are constant **2018 PPP dollars per person**. The government-spending model is the first public-financing sensitivity; the total-spending model is a separate broader-resource sensitivity. These choices are made before either fit. Do not put both expenditure variables or the FY2015 income indicator into these models, select one by p-value, add interactions, alter the outbreak window, or change the sample.

`β_B,h` is the conditional country-month log-odds association for two countries differing by one unit of 66-month mean recorded log activity at the same monthly deviation and **same modeled 2013 per-person expenditure**. `β_W,h` is the within-country monthly-deviation association conditional on that country's mean and expenditure, but this pooled model is **not** country fixed effects. `γ_h` is a descriptive between-country conditional association, not a causal effect of expenditure or a direct estimate of OCV-request capacity. A one-unit `H` difference is a one-unit difference in `ln(1+PPP spending)`; the odds ratio is `exp(γ_h)`.

## Data provenance and timing

Source is the user-supplied CSV and DTA from the [IHME GHDx 2019 release](https://ghdx.healthdata.org/record/ihme-data/global-health-spending-1995-2016) and its accompanying information sheet. The release is retrospective, covers 1995–2016, and was **updated September 30, 2019 after errors in the initial estimates**. The information sheet defines the two per-capita variables and their lower/upper uncertainty intervals. The linked [2019 Lancet methods article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6548764/) describes government, out-of-pocket and prepaid private as separate domestic sources; total health spending additionally includes development assistance for health net of agency administration. Government includes social and mandated private insurance as well as public health programmes. GHED inputs exclude major investment; IHME uses spatiotemporal Gaussian-process regression, so a complete 2013 value is **not** proof of a direct country-year observation. PPP conversion uses the study's GDP PPP series.

The 2013 calendar-year reference precedes the panel start, but the *estimate* was released and revised later. It is not a contemporaneously observed decision-maker resource measure. South Sudan appears in the dataset back to 1995; the source files do not include a country-year observed-versus-imputed flag. Retain its estimate with this qualification rather than treating it as verified national accounts, setting it to zero, or patching it into a WHO/World Bank series. IHME and WHO values are distinct vintages/estimation systems and must not be combined into one variable.

## Fixed inference and validation gates

Match the frozen implementation: statsmodels binomial GLM with country-cluster sandwich covariance (`use_correction=False`); report log-odds coefficients, odds ratios and normal asymptotic 95% CIs. For **the between and within outbreak-activity coefficients only**, use the exact frozen score-linearized restricted WCLR-C logistic wild-cluster bootstrap, 99,999 Rademacher repetitions and fixed seeds 20261128 and 20261129, respectively. Its observed statistic uses CV1 studentization, while the displayed CIs use the uncorrected cluster sandwich. Do not label those CIs bootstrap intervals. The expenditure coefficient is descriptive; do not make its p-value a model-selection criterion.

Before accepting either adjusted result, require: (1) 1,650 unique country-months, 25 countries × 66 ordered months, 58 event-months; (2) an exact ISO3 one-to-one join to **2013 Country-level** IHME records, with no missing government/total means or uncertainty bounds; (3) DTA and CSV agreement and unchanged original 21 panel columns; (4) 2013 expenditure repeated identically across each country's 66 months, numeric/nonnegative, `H=ln(1+x)` verified independently; (5) direct outbreak-interval reconstruction of all 1,650 prior-12-month counts, plus `Z=ln(1+A)`, `B+W=Z`, constant `B`, mean-zero `W`; (6) an unadjusted same-panel GLM that reproduces frozen coefficients, CIs and AIC; (7) full-rank, well-conditioned design with country-level overlap and no separation or nonconvergence; (8) finite predictions and coefficient/CIs; (9) source hashes, support diagnostics, country-level correlations and all 25 one-country-deleted descriptive coefficients. If a gate fails, stop rather than searching transformations or exclusions to rescue a result.

The between-country information resides in 25 clusters, despite 1,650 rows and 58 events. Country deletions diagnose leverage; they are not alternative headline samples. Government and total expenditure are expected to correlate strongly and are never entered together. The four countries with zero 12-month predictor throughout have no within-predictor variation. Recorded outbreak activity, surveillance/reporting, request opportunity, political/partner support and expenditure may share upstream causes. National-month aggregation, binary outcome, retrospective record intervals, and the 66-month country mean preclude outbreak-specific, causal, propensity-per-outbreak, delay, or real-time capacity claims. Logistic coefficient changes do not quantify a proportion “explained” because odds ratios are noncollapsible ([Schuster et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34225653/)).

Decision rule: report **both** prespecified expenditure sensitivities alongside the untouched frozen benchmark regardless of sign or significance, provided gates pass. Compare coefficient stability descriptively, not as evidence that a particular capacity mechanism has been isolated. Supporting references: [Zheng et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35605949/) for recorded outbreak data, [MacKinnon, Nielsen & Webb](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07474938.2025.2515161) for clustered logistic bootstrap, and [Courvoisier et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21411281/) for sparse-data cautions.
