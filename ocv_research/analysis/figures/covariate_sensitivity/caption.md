Baseline-covariate sensitivity of between- and within-country associations with reactive OCV request-months. Each model contains both components of logged recorded outbreak activity in the preceding 12 months. Diamonds show the primary model estimates; circles show estimates after adding one country-level baseline covariate separately. Government and total health expenditure are alternative models, not jointly entered covariates. All models use the same 25 countries and 1,650 country-months, including 58 reactive request-months. The prior-campaign model uses the corrected seven-country documented-history indicator, including Kalemie, DR Congo, in November 2013. Coefficients are per one-unit increase in the relevant log-transformed predictor component, not per standard deviation. Whiskers are asymptotic 95% confidence intervals based on country-clustered standard errors. Dashed colored lines mark the corresponding primary coefficient; the gray vertical line marks zero. The change columns subtract the primary point estimate from each adjusted point estimate; they are not causal covariate effects or confidence intervals/tests for differences between models. Panel x-axis ranges differ. Wild-cluster-bootstrap p-values are reported separately in the sensitivity table.
