# Targeted historical audit of countries currently coded zero

Date: 13 October 2026. Input: `country_prior_ocv_campaign_pre2014jul.csv` (19 zero-coded countries). No lookup, source ledger, panel or fitted model was modified. No sensitivity was rerun.

## Eligibility and search boundaries

Qualifying evidence means actual vaccine administration during a mass oral cholera vaccination campaign within the country before 1 July 2014, irrespective of campaign purpose or funding source. A pre-cutoff outbreak, vaccine recommendation, acceptability survey, modelled hypothetical campaign, request, approval or shipment alone does not qualify. A small immunogenicity trial does not establish mass campaign experience. Interrupted campaigns count if vaccines were administered. Refugee campaigns are assigned by implementation country, not recipient nationality. Publication may be later than 2014 if it describes an earlier implemented campaign.

Targeted web searches used country names with oral cholera vaccine/vaccination, campaign, first, and 2010–2014 date terms; French variants were used for Benin, Burundi, Chad, Niger and Congo. Côte d'Ivoire/Ivory Coast and Republic of the Congo/Brazzaville were distinguished from DR Congo. Backward checks used the Martin et al. 2014 deployment review, WHO SAGE 2017 inventory, and Pezzoli 2020 stockpile account. Some PMC pages were blocked; primary-author abstracts or official campaign reports were used where available. This is not an exhaustive multilingual systematic review and negative search results do not verify absence.

## Decision before rerunning

Only DR Congo currently has verified qualifying evidence requiring a 0-to-1 change. No additional qualifying positive was found among the other 18 zero-coded countries in this targeted audit. Several zeros remain an ascertainment category, not verified negatives. Do not present them as countries that never used OCV.

With the known correction alone, there would be seven documented-positive countries and 18 countries with no qualifying campaign documented. That statement should remain conditional on the six existing positive-country records, which this zero-country audit was not designed to revalidate comprehensively.

Before executing a rerun: preserve the original lookup/ledger and output provenance, add Massing evidence, correct the Ethiopia timeline note, and ensure the Methods/Table S10 wording continues to define zero as no campaign documented in reviewed sources. Do not broaden the variable to any prior cholera outbreak or any individual/trial vaccination. Frozen main estimates remain untouched.
